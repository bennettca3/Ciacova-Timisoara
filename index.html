Ciacova to Timișoara

Bus route

I taught English as a Peace Corps Volunteer in the village of Ciacova, Romania. My classes consisted of middle school and high school students.

This is the route that I would take to the largest city near my site. The bus ran three times a day to Timișoara. I could walk to the train station or the city center from there. Romania is a gorgeous country and it can be easily travelled by bus or train.
